This is the home page for the Andrades Valtueña lab at the [Institute of Evolutionary Biology](https://www.ibe.upf-csic.es/) (IBE, [CSIC](http://www.csic.es/)-[UPF](https://www.upf.edu/)) in Barcelona, Spain.

The research focus of our lab is on the study of ancient pathogen to understand the emergence and the long-term evolution and adaptation of pathogens in the context of anthropogenic change. We employ ancient DNA techniques to recover the genomes of ancient pathogen from historical or archaeological samples (e.g. archeological human remains). We also optimise bioinformatic workflows to the nature of ancient DNA to unlock past genomic diversity of pathogens. Ancient pathogen genomes serve as deep-time data points to trace the emergence of pathogenicity and their evolutionary histories.
